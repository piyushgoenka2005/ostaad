LEGAL / PRIVACY

Privacy, plainly.

LAST UPDATED: 14 SEPTEMBER 2026

Ostaad is designed to make construction information clearer. We collect only the information needed to respond to a project enquiry and operate this website.

Information you share

When you use the contact form, you may provide your name, email address, project type, approximate area and message. We use this information to respond to your enquiry and understand your project context.

Cookies and browser storage

Ostaad stores your cookie preference locally in your browser under the key ostaad-cookie-preference. Essential storage remembers that choice. Optional cookies are not enabled unless you choose "Accept all". You can reset your cookie choice at any time by clearing your browser's local storage. No tracking cookies are set by this site.

How we use information

We use enquiry information to communicate with you about Ostaad and your project. We do not sell personal information.

Your choices

You can clear browser storage at any time through your browser settings. You can also contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information you shared.

Contact

For privacy questions, use our contact page.
