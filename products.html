Grade 53 vs Grade 43 Cement — Which one where?

Understanding which cement grade to deploy avoids over-expenditure in masonry while ensuring certified structural integrity for RCC framing.

TECHNICAL PARAMETER OPC GRADE 53 OPC GRADE 43 PPC (BLENDED)
28-Day Strength ≥ 53 MPa ≥ 43 MPa ≥ 33 MPa (Long term gain)
Heat of Hydration High (Requires quick curing) Moderate Low (Minimal micro-cracking)
Ideal Structural Zone Columns, Beams, Cantilevers, Slabs Brick Masonry, Boundary Walls Plastering, Foundation Mass Concrete
De-shuttering Period Faster (3–7 Days depending on span) Standard (7–14 Days) Standard (10–14 Days)
Approximate Rate ₹385 / Bag ₹360 / Bag ₹345 / Bag
